# Computer Networks submission checklist (V3)

Checked against the journal's Guide for Authors on 2026-11-26: LaTeX submissions may use double-column layout; a PDF alone is not an acceptable source file.

Submit at https://submit.elsevier.com/COMNET. The ready-to-upload package is in submission/ (see submission/UPLOAD_STEPS.md).

Upload:
1. Manuscript source: cn.tex (two-column elsarticle 5p, 19 pages) with refs.bib and the figures/ folder. Let the system build the PDF, or also upload cn.pdf.
2. Highlights: cn_highlights.txt, as a separate file with "highlights" in the file name (3 to 5 bullets, each at most 85 characters; ours: 5 bullets, longest 83).
3. CRediT statement: cn_credit_statement.txt (single author, all roles).
4. Declaration of competing interest: in the manuscript; the system may also ask for the form.
5. Declaration of generative AI use: in the manuscript; declare it again in the submission form.
6. Funding statement and data availability statement: in the manuscript (repository link included).
7. Abstract: 249 words (limit 250). Keywords: 7 (the maximum).
8. Optional: graphical abstract and cover letter (encouraged, not required).
9. Publication route: choose subscription (no article publishing charge); open access would carry a personalized APC.

One-column copy for reference: cn_onecolumn.tex (34 pages), same content.

The journal also asks for a biography of at most 100 words and a passport-type photo for each author, as separate editable files. Not prepared yet; Sameh to supply.
